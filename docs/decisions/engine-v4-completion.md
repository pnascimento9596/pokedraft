Boss difficulty remains an explicit calibrated ladder value. Actual XI role fits adjust line shape
by at most three points around that ladder centre. Formation, theme, captain and tactical label
come from the resolved XI. Cities do not imply an undocumented canonical Pokémon type or region.
The formation is explicitly 4-3-3. The engine does not claim globally monotonic raw XI attributes
across independently sampled type pools. Difficulty progresses through the calibrated ladder.

The F=980 baseline slightly exceeded the established 900/950 flawless targets. F=990 overcorrected
the 950 target. F=985 meets all existing interval targets, with a maximum ladder step of 130.
Old engine 3 calibration remains historical evidence and was not relabeled as engine 4.

The first combined browser selection exposed a species-data download during challenge load.
RoadStrip imported full roster generation. Extracting the seeded, data-free schedule preserves
names, ratings and live replay hashes while restoring the pre-draft boundary.
